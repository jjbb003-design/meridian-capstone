# Confidentiality paragraph — draft for Joseph to review

I can use Meridian's sales totals by store and week, along with store attributes such as square footage and opening dates, with AI because the client brief explicitly allows those categories. I cannot put customer records, loyalty membership or purchase histories, employee information, labor schedules, or excerpts of those records into any AI tool; removing names would not make them permitted. If a file mixes weekly sales totals with loyalty or employee data, I would keep the original out of AI and have an authorized person or approved non-AI process create and inspect a separate extract containing only allowed fields, including checking hidden sheets and comments. I would ask Meridian about any data category the brief does not clearly permit, and I would not assume permission to use data with AI also permits publishing it in a public GitHub repository.

Source checked by Codex: [Meridian client brief, Terms of engagement](https://github.com/LMU-MSBA/bsan-6088-fa26/blob/main/workshops/client-brief.md#terms-of-engagement).
