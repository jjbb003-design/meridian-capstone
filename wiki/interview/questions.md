# October 16 stakeholder interview guide

Prepared for Joseph Barragan. Questions below are proposed by Codex for Joseph to review and prioritize. They are not claims that the interview occurred.

## Suggested opening

“We understand you want clearer store and category performance to support expansion. Before building the dashboard, we'd like to confirm the decision it needs to support, how you will judge success, and what data we may use.”

## Dana — highest-priority questions

1. **Decision:** What specific decision must the board make, by what date, and who has final authority? What is essential for the preliminary version versus the eight-week deliverable?
2. **Avoid a predetermined answer:** What would make you reject Pasadena, and which alternatives—including delaying expansion—should we compare?
3. **Success:** If revenue, cost reduction, and customer experience point in different directions, which takes priority? What metric, baseline, and target would count as success?
4. **Comparable stores:** Which stores are fair benchmarks for a new location? Should we account for store age, selling area, closures, and unusual events before ranking sales?
5. **Format:** Which parts of Meridian's smaller footprint, prepared-food offering, and local sourcing are non-negotiable? Could they vary by neighborhood?
6. **Local evidence:** What candidate-site information already exists about demand, competition, parking, access, rent, build-out costs, and existing-store overlap?

Why these questions: the [brief](https://github.com/LMU-MSBA/bsan-6088-fa26/blob/main/workshops/client-brief.md) leaves success criteria, site comparisons, and the exact board deadline unresolved. [Public expansion research](../synthesis/expansion.md) supplies examples but not a Meridian recommendation. [SafeGraph](https://www.safegraph.com/blog/retail-site-selection-checklist/) provides a broader evaluation checklist.

## Marcus — data questions after NDA and authorization

- Can you provide store-week sales totals and store attributes as a separate, inspected, approved extract?
- What changed in the POS migration: dates, store IDs, category mappings, returns, taxes, or missing periods? Is a reconciliation available?
- How are “sales” and reporting weeks defined, and what control totals can validate the extract?
- Does the client authorize category-level aggregates for AI use? The requested dashboard needs categories, but the brief only explicitly allows store-week sales totals and store attributes.
- Who confirms data quality and authorizes future extracts? Customer and employee information must stay out of all AI tools.

## Close the interview

Read back the agreed decision, measures, deadline, allowed data, owners, and next action. Send a recap only after Joseph authorizes sending it. Record unresolved points rather than interpreting silence as agreement.
