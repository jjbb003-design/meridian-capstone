# Site-selection questions beyond sales

SafeGraph recommends combining local demand, competition, access, footprint, and financial feasibility, with physical validation. [Original checklist](https://www.safegraph.com/blog/retail-site-selection-checklist/); [source notes](../../raw/safegraph-site-selection.md).

**Limitation:** This is vendor guidance. Its examples and AI claims were not treated as independently validated results.

**Inference for Meridian:** A store-sales dashboard is a starting point; site comparison also needs evidence about candidate locations. Client data boundaries still apply to any analysis.
