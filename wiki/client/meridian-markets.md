# Meridian Markets — fictional client

Source: Dana Okafor's August 2026 [client brief](https://github.com/LMU-MSBA/bsan-6088-fa26/blob/main/workshops/client-brief.md), preserved [locally](../../raw/client-brief.md). All client facts below come from this brief, not independent verification of a real company.

## Situation

Meridian operates 14 specialty grocery stores across Los Angeles, Orange, and Ventura counties, with roughly $78 million annual revenue and about 620 employees. It emphasizes prepared foods, local sourcing, and smaller stores. It grew from six to 14 stores in five years, largely by taking over vacated leases. [Brief: About us](https://github.com/LMU-MSBA/bsan-6088-fa26/blob/main/workshops/client-brief.md#about-us).

## Decision and requested output

Dana requests a dashboard of sales by store and category to support the next location decision. She favors Pasadena, but the brief provides no comparative site evidence establishing it as best. Growth has been uneven. Revenue growth, operating costs, and customer experience are all goals; their priority and measurable targets remain unspecified. [Brief: What we need and success](https://github.com/LMU-MSBA/bsan-6088-fa26/blob/main/workshops/client-brief.md#what-we-need).

## Data and restrictions

The brief lists approximately three years of POS transactions, loyalty information, labor scheduling/hours, and store attributes. A POS migration occurred last spring; Marcus can arrange an extract after the NDA. Only store-week sales totals and store attributes are explicitly permitted for AI. Customer and employee records, including excerpts, are prohibited. [Brief: Data and terms](https://github.com/LMU-MSBA/bsan-6088-fa26/blob/main/workshops/client-brief.md#data-we-have).

## Timing and communication

The brief describes roughly eight weeks for the engagement and a preliminary board presentation in three weeks; those are relative to the brief, not newly confirmed calendar deadlines. Dana prefers email and is slow to reply while traveling Tuesdays and Wednesdays. Confirm actual milestone dates in the interview. [Brief: Timeline](https://github.com/LMU-MSBA/bsan-6088-fa26/blob/main/workshops/client-brief.md#timeline).

## Analytical questions — Codex interpretation

- What decision must the board make, and how should success be measured?
- Which alternative sites and a no-expansion option should be compared with Pasadena?
- How should store age and area affect fair performance comparisons?
- Did the POS migration change category definitions, transaction coverage, or return treatment?
- Can Marcus supply explicitly permitted aggregates? Category-level data is requested for the dashboard but is not explicitly named in the AI permission; clarify before ingestion.

These are discovery questions, not findings. No store rankings, customer profiles, or profitability estimates are available from the brief.
